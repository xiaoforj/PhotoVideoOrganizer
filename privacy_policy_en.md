# Photo & Video Organizer - Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 24, 2026

## 1. Overview

"Photo & Video Organizer" (hereinafter referred to as "the App") is developed by Iris_Xiao. We value your privacy, and this Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## 2. Data Collection

The App **does not collect** any personal data. All data processing is performed locally on your device and is not uploaded to any server.

### 2.1 Locally Processed Data
The following data is processed only on your local device and is not transmitted externally:
- Photo and video file metadata (EXIF information, GPS coordinates, etc.)
- Face feature vectors (used for face recognition)
- File hash values (used for deduplication)
- User settings and preferences

### 2.2 Data Not Collected
The App **does not** collect the following data:
- Photo and video content itself
- Location information
- Device information
- Usage statistics
- Any personally identifiable information

## 3. Data Storage

### 3.1 Local Storage
- Face profile data is stored in a local SQLite database
- User settings are stored in a local JSON configuration file
- All data is stored only on your device

### 3.2 Data Deletion
- You can remove face data at any time by deleting face profiles
- Uninstalling the app will delete all app data

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to users through in-app notifications.
